Partnership FAQs

What documents are required to activate store partnership? expand_more

You'll need a valid shop establishment proof (or Kirana registration license), owner PAN card, cancelled check for settlements, and GST registration if applicable.

How long does the setup take before listing products? expand_more

Our relationship team typically audits documents and launches store catalogs on our customer app within 24 to 48 hours of onboarding form submission.

Who delivers the orders to customer doorsteps? expand_more

QuickBazar handles all logistics. Our dedicated riders pick up packed grocery bags directly from your shop counter, allowing you to focus purely on packing fresh stock!
