Frequently Answered Help Guides

How long does a cancelled order refund take? expand_more

For transactions cancelled within our 60-second absolute window, refunds are settled instantly to your QuickBazar Wallet. For UPI/Card bank channels, settlements reflect in 2 to 3 operational banking days.

My 10-minute delivery is delayed. What should I do? expand_more

Severe rainfall or local street surges occasionally affect transit times. You can track your rider Amit's live location in the order tracking widget. If your order exceeds 30 minutes, you will receive a 50% discount credit coupon automatically!

Can I modify my delivery address for an active order? expand_more

To keep our 10-minute delivery promise, orders are locked for address modifications once the local dark store partner completes packing (typically 90 seconds post order). Please contact live chat immediately if you need rider re-routing.
